Support

Arc Squads · last updated 27 August 2026

Arc Squads is a challenge app. You pick an arc, set the daily rules you want to live by, and run it for 30, 60 or 90 days — on your own or with up to eleven mates.

Any question, problem or bug, write to us and a person will answer:

hello@arcsquads.com

We aim to reply within two working days. If you are reporting a bug, telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps us find it faster.

Getting started

What is an arc?

An arc is a set of daily rules with an end date. There are 50 to choose from, covering training, sport, study, growth, mindset and everyday life. Each one arrives already written, at three levels of commitment, so you can start in about a minute.

Can I change the rules?

Yes. Every rule can be edited, swapped or removed, and you can write your own. Nothing in an arc is fixed once you have picked it.

How long does an arc run?

30, 60 or 90 days. Ninety is the default, because that is long enough to stop being a phase.

What is a rest day?

Days you choose not to train. Rest days pause the rules that need pausing and leave the rest running, so a rest day never reads as a missed day. You still tick something on a rest day for it to count.

Squads

How do I start one, or join one?

Open the Squad tab. Starting a squad gives you an invite code; joining one means typing in a mate's code. Up to twelve people can be in a squad, each running their own arc with their own rules.

What can my mates actually see?

Only which days you showed up. Not what your rules are, not how many you set, not which ones you ticked or missed. This is not a setting you have to find and switch on — it is how the app is built, and it cannot be turned off. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored and shared.

What is the squad streak?

A day counts for the squad when everyone whose arc has started shows up that day. Someone joining later never resets it — days before they arrived are carried by the people who were already running.

What is the ladder?

A ranking of who has shown up most over the last seven days, tiebroken by current streak. It counts days, never what you ticked, so adding another rule to your plan can never cost you a place. A mate who has just joined sits below the ladder until they have had as many days as everyone else.

What is an ember?

A single tap that tells a mate you noticed. One per person per day. There is no feed, no chat and no comments in Arc Squads, on purpose.

Someone in my squad is behaving badly

Press and hold their card in the Squad tab to block or report them. Blocking is immediate and mutual. Reports come to us and we read every one.

Notifications

Arc Squads sends you nothing until you ask it to. There is no daily reminder, no streak warning and no notification of any kind unless you turn one on yourself in Settings. If you do want a nudge, you choose the time.

Subscriptions and payment

What do I get for free?

You can browse the whole library, run an arc and be in a squad. The free version caps you at four active rules at a time.

What does a subscription add?

It removes the cap, so you can run as many rules as you want. It is available monthly, yearly, or as a one-off lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy anything.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next payment. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

I want a refund

Refunds are also handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. We cannot issue refunds on Apple's behalf, but if something went wrong please tell us as well so we can fix it.

I paid and nothing unlocked

Open Settings inside Arc Squads and tap Restore purchases. That re-checks your Apple account. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we will sort it out.

Your account and your data

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and choose to delete your account. This removes your profile, your arc, your check-ins and your squad membership. It cannot be undone. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Who can use Arc Squads?

Anyone aged 13 or over. The app is not built for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

What do you store about me?

The privacy policy lists it in full, including what crash reports do and do not carry.
