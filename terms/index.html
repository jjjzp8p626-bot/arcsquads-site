Paid: removes the four-rule cap and unlocks the rest of the app.

We offer paid access in more than one shape — a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, and a one-off lifetime purchase. Which of these are available, and what each one costs in your currency, is shown to you on the subscription screen in the app before you pay. Apple sets the prices you see in your local currency.

We never quote a price on this page. Prices change, and they differ by country. The price you see in the app, in your own currency, on the screen where you tap to buy, is the real one. That screen also states the length of the subscription and the length of any free trial before you commit.

4. Free trials

Sometimes a free trial is offered on a subscription. When one is available, the app tells you how long it lasts before you start it.

Two things worth knowing about trials, because they catch people out:

  • A trial turns into a paid subscription automatically when it ends, unless you cancel before then.
  • Trials are once per person, not once per subscription. Apple decides whether your Apple Account is still eligible for one. If Apple says you are not eligible, the app will not offer you a trial.

To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

5. Subscriptions renew automatically

Subscriptions renew by themselves until you cancel. That is how Apple's subscriptions work, and it applies here too.

  • Your Apple Account is charged when you confirm the purchase.
  • It renews within 24 hours before the current period ends, at the same price, unless you cancel.
  • Cancelling stops the next charge. It does not cut off the time you have already paid for — you keep your access until the period runs out.

A lifetime purchase is different: you pay once and it does not renew.

6. How to cancel

You cancel through Apple, not through us. We cannot cancel a subscription on your behalf, because we never handle the payment.

On your iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Arc Squads → Cancel Subscription.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Neither does deleting your Arc Squads account. You have to cancel in the place above, or you will keep being charged.

8. Squad rules — how to treat other people

A squad is a small group of people who can see each other's check-ins and progress. That only works if it stays a decent place to be.

Do not:

  • Use a handle or squad name that is offensive, hateful, sexual, or pretends to be someone else.
  • Post anything illegal, or anything that encourages someone to harm themselves.
  • Share other people's private information.
  • Use the app to advertise, spam, or sell things.
  • Try to break, overload, or reverse-engineer the app.

We filter obvious profanity in handles and squad names automatically, but a filter is not a substitute for behaving decently.

If someone is out of line

You can block them, which stops you seeing each other, and you can report them, which tells us. Both are in the app.

Reports come to a real person. We may remove content, remove someone from a squad, or close an account entirely. Serious things — threats, content involving children — we report to the authorities.

If you think we have got a decision wrong, email hello@arcsquads.com and we will look again.
